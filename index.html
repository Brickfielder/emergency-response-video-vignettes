Safety Vignettes

Before you begin

A clinician-led way to explore everyday safety judgement.

Use short video vignettes, supported visual choices, and structured notes to observe how someone identifies risks, reasons about consequences, and chooses what to do next.

This app does not diagnose, score, pass, fail, or replace clinical judgement. Interpret responses alongside functional observation, communication needs, cognition, fatigue, collateral history, and MDT discussion.

Session flow

  1. Choose a scenario and play the video, replaying or pausing if needed.
  2. Ask open clinical questions before offering image choices.
  3. Record prompts, selections, and context before exporting the summary.
